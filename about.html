About the College

History, vision and mission

ABOUT THE COLLEGE

Langat Singh College, Muzaffarpur

Langat Singh College, popularly known as L. S. College, was established on July 3, 1899. The college has a long history in the educational, literary, social and political life of Bihar.

The institution was affiliated to Calcutta University in 1900, declared a Government College in 1915 and later affiliated to Patna University in 1917. After Bihar University was established at Muzaffarpur in 1952, the college became affiliated to the new university.

The college's stated objective includes developing creativity, tolerance and scientific temper while responding to changing social and economic needs.

VISION

Vision

Empowering youth for capacity building, inculcating basic moral values, community development and fair access for poor and socially disadvantaged groups.

MISSION

Mission

To equip students with a modern world view, team spirit, independent and original thinking so that they can become pioneers in their own fields.

COLLEGE HISTORY

A Heritage Institution

The official college history notes its Indo-Saracenic architectural character and describes the building as modelled after Balliol College of Oxford.

The college also records its historic association with Mahatma Gandhi's visit to the campus on April 11, 1917, on his way to the Champaran Satyagraha.
